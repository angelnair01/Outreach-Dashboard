Privacy policy

OpenGrad Outreach Dashboard · last updated 15 November 2026

What this dashboard is

A private tool used by the OpenGrad team to follow up on outreach emails. It shows each signed-in person the outreach they sent, the replies they received and their upcoming follow-ups.

What we receive when you sign in with Google

Only your Google account's basic profile: your email address, your name and your profile picture. We do not get your password and we do not get access to your Gmail, Drive or any other Google data through this sign-in.

How it is used

  • Your email address is used to show you only your own outreach records from the team's Google Sheet.
  • When you add a contact or log outreach on the dashboard, it is saved in that Google Sheet together with your email address, so it appears for you.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Where data is stored

Outreach records are kept in a Google Sheet owned by OpenGrad and read through a Google Apps Script. Your browser keeps a temporary copy of your own data and your sign-in so pages load quickly; Sign out in Settings removes them from that browser.

Deleting your data

Ask the OpenGrad team (the person who invited you to the dashboard) and your records will be removed from the sheet.
